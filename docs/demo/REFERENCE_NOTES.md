# Reference Video Review

**Reference:** `docs/demo/reference/reference-video.mp4` (operator-supplied, 72.4s, 1108×720, 30 fps, H.264/AAC).

## Structural observations only

- Screen-recorded SaaS product demo; no human presenter on camera.
- Starts on the signed-in workspace, then moves through sign-in and product screens.
- Fast pacing: short dwell per screen, direct cuts, frequent scroll/hover movement.
- Bold caption overlays (large, high-contrast phrases) reinforce the narration.
- One continuous male narrator; conversational, confident tone.
- Minimal or no music bed; the voice and UI clicks carry the track.
- Text density is low: one product idea per scene, with the UI doing the proof.
- Ends on a product/positioning card rather than a presenter.

## Adopted

- Short screen-recorded product walkthrough with a single male narrator.
- Direct cuts and brief dwell per screen; scroll/hover movement to show the app is live.
- A final evidence card with the concrete contract, transaction, run and revocation facts.
- Neutral third-person narration, no presenter name or personal identity.

## Rejected / not copied

- No footage, frames, music, logos, presenter likeness or verbatim narration from the reference.
- No copied caption wording, visual brand, colours or UI composition.
- No health/finance product content; the reference was used only for pacing and structure.

## Precedence and limits

The hard limits in `docs/DEMO_VIDEO.md` override the reference: maximum 3:00, free Google AI Studio male voice, no presenter name, every claim demonstrable. The final video is 2:02 and uses a free male neural voice; the Google AI Studio voice requirement is recorded as a Known Limitation because no Google AI Studio credential was supplied.
